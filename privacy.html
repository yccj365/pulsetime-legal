PulseTime Pro

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026  ·  Last updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PulseTime Pro ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is a habit and routine reminder application for iOS and Android, developed by an independent developer.

In short: PulseTime Pro does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your own device.

1. Information we do not collect

The App requires no account and no sign-in. We do not collect or receive:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Your location or movement data
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone, or camera data
  • Advertising identifiers or device fingerprints
  • Usage analytics, session recordings, or behavioural profiles

2. Information stored on your device

To make the App work, the following is stored locally in the App's private storage on your device:

  • Habit names, schedules, repeat days, and reminder settings
  • Completion records and streak counts
  • App preferences such as theme, language, and reminder sound choice
  • The date your free trial started

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded to any server, and we have no ability to access it. Deleting the App, or using Settings → Reset all data inside the App, permanently removes it.

3. Notifications

The App schedules local notifications directly on your device. Notification permission is optional and can be granted or revoked at any time in your system settings. Notifications are generated on-device; no notification content is sent through our servers.

4. In-app purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive, see, or store your payment card details, billing address, or full name. The store only provides the App with a confirmation that a subscription is active, which is stored locally on your device.

Your purchase is also governed by the privacy policy of the store you used:

  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The App contains no analytics SDK, no advertising SDK, no social login, and no third-party tracking libraries. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not use your data for advertising.

6. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and CCPA give you rights to access, correct, export, and delete your personal data. Because we do not collect or store personal data on any server, there is no server-side record for us to access, correct, export, or delete.

You remain in full control of the data on your device:

  • Delete individual habits inside the App
  • Use Settings → Reset all data to erase everything
  • Uninstall the App to remove all associated local data

8. International users

The App is available worldwide. Because your data is never transmitted off your device, there is no international transfer of personal data by us.

9. Data security

Your data is protected by the security model of your device's operating system, including app sandboxing and device encryption. Please keep your device passcode enabled and your system up to date.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact
